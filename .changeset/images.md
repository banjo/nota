Images and embedded notes: `![](image.png)` and `![[image.png|300]]` show the image from the vault, and `![[Note]]` or `![[Note#Heading]]` embeds the note. Pasting or dropping an image saves it in `Attachments/` and links it; images sync like notes. Images from the web are never loaded. See docs/markdown.md.
