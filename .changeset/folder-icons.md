Folders and saved views can have an icon and a colour. Right-click a folder (or a view) and choose "Change icon…" to search 12,000 icons from Lucide, Tabler and Simple Icons, or an emoji, and pick a colour that follows the theme. The icons sync with the vault (`.nota/folders.yaml`) and follow a renamed folder. `nota folder icon Work lucide:briefcase --color blue` and `nota icons <words>` do the same from the terminal, and `set_folder_icon` and `search_icons` over MCP.
