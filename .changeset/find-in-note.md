Find and replace in a note. ⌘F opens a find bar over the note with match case, whole word and regex toggles, a match count, and Enter / ⇧Enter to step through the matches; ⌥⌘F adds a replace row with Replace and All. It works in Live, Source and Reading (find only), in the desktop app and on the web, and on a phone from the note's "…" sheet.
